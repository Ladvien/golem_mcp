## How the UV was done

Rather than one island per face, I marked a real seam set to unfold the box into a **single cross island**: cut one vertical corner (opening the four sides into a strip) plus three top and three bottom edges (leaving the top and bottom flaps attached to the +X side). The chamfer strips and corner triangles were seam-split so each stays attached to its correct face instead of becoming orphan islands. Result: 1 island, 0 overlapping faces, 0 faces outside the 0–1 square.

One note for your awareness: the cross layout's UV coverage is ~0.39 — that's inherent to a cross shape (the four empty corner regions of the cross), not a defect. If you'd like higher texel density, I could split it into a few islands and pack them tighter, but that would trade away the single-island cross. Let me know if you want that.
